## Narrative and measurement rules

- Every traveler reaches the same scenes in the same order. Choices produce evidence, never side quests.
- Every option has a plausible benefit and cost. Directional evidence is an internal design aid, not public copy.
- Signal maps stay in server-only modules. The public manifest includes identity, status and language-neutral asset declarations only.
- Images are existing internal placeholders marked `pending`. They contain no readable language and are not approved production assets.
- This is a reflective Questype construct model. It is not a clinical diagnosis or a validated psychometric scale.

   Benefit / Beneficio: Coordinates the room with less social exposure. / Coordina la sala con menor exposición social.  
   Cost / Costo: May appear distant or overly procedural at a symbolic moment. / Puede parecer distante o demasiado procedimental en un momento simbólico.  
   Internal evidence intent: + leadership_initiative/responsibility_assumption @ public_visibility; − social_confidence/interpersonal_visibility @ public_visibility
3. **Speak clearly about the danger of delay, but leave the order of discussion to the senior delegate. / Hablas con claridad sobre el peligro de la demora, pero dejas el orden de discusión al delegado de mayor rango.**  
   Benefit / Beneficio: Makes a position visible without taking full control. / Hace visible una posición sin tomar el control completo.  
   Cost / Costo: Relies on status to produce coordination. / Depende del rango para producir coordinación.  
   Internal evidence intent: − leadership_initiative/responsibility_assumption @ public_visibility; + social_confidence/interpersonal_visibility @ public_visibility

   Benefit / Beneficio: Avoids creating an illegitimate precedent. / Evita crear un precedente ilegítimo.
